## Declaration of Original Concept

StreamVista Cloud X is an original business concept and platform architecture conceived by **Abijith U A**, associated with **Crayons Pictures** and **StreamVista OPC Private Limited**. The concept is intended to transform the way films, television content, series, documentaries, digital media, and other professional audio-visual assets are licensed, commercially managed, securely delivered, reported, and renewed through a controlled business-to-business environment.

StreamVista Cloud X is not intended to operate as a public consumer movie marketplace. Its defining model is a **confidential B2B Content Licensing, Rights Management, Deal Negotiation, Payment, Secure Delivery, Revenue Operations, and Renewal Platform** connecting verified content owners with verified commercial buyers.

The content-owner side may include filmmakers, producers, production houses, studios, distributors, copyright owners, and other authorised rights holders. The buyer side may include OTT platforms, broadcasters, satellite channels, FAST channels, digital streaming services, theatrical and non-theatrical distributors, airlines, hospitality networks, educational institutions, cultural organisations, telecom platforms, and approved AI/ML licensing partners.

## StreamVista as Master Distributor and Commercial Controller

A central feature of the StreamVista concept is that **StreamVista Admin acts as the Master Distributor and Commercial Controller**. The platform is designed so that commercial discussions, title evaluation, rights verification, screener approval, offers, counter-offers, negotiations, agreements, payment supervision, delivery, revenue reconciliation, payouts, and renewals occur through confidential, permission-controlled workflows inside the StreamVista system.

Creators and buyers are not intended to bypass the platform through uncontrolled direct commercial transactions unless StreamVista explicitly authorises such contact. StreamVista therefore serves not merely as a software listing service, but as the controlled operational layer between rights owners and licensed buyers.

## End-to-End Business Architecture

The intended lifecycle is:

Content Owner → Create Title → Metadata and Asset Preparation → Rights and Legal Documents → Submission → Admin Intake Review → Technical QC → Rights and Legal Review → Approved → Ready for Distribution → Commercial Profile and Rights Validation → Confidential Buyer Marketplace → Buyer Discovery → Screener Request → Admin Approval → Secure Screener → Deal Room → Negotiation → Agreement and Digital Signing → Invoice and Payment → License Activation → Secure Cloud Delivery → Buyer Acceptance → Buyer Platform Release Tracking → Revenue Reporting → Creator Revenue Statement → StreamVista Fees / Commission → Creator Payout → Renewal, Extension, Amendment, Expiry, or Termination.

The administrative distribution-readiness state is **ready_for_distribution**. This state means that a title has satisfied StreamVista’s internal metadata, QC, legal, rights, and commercial-readiness checks. It does not mean that the title has been published by a buyer. Actual release should be tracked per buyer, deal, territory, platform, language, and licensing window, using a deal-level state such as **live_on_buyer_platform** or an equivalent release record.

## Rights, Territory, Language and Licensing Architecture

The StreamVista concept supports flexible rights and licensing structures rather than a single global licence. Rights may include OTT, streaming, SVOD, AVOD, TVOD, FAST, satellite, free television, pay television, theatrical, airline, hospitality, educational, festival, clip, promotional, dubbing, subtitle, remake, adaptation, mobile, telecom, archival, and specifically authorised AI/ML rights.

Each licence may be scoped by territory, language, exclusivity, platform, term, window, holdback, restrictions, sub-licensing permissions, pricing model, and reporting requirements. AI/ML rights are not assumed from general digital rights and should require explicit written authorisation from the rights holder.

Commercial models may include fixed licence fees, minimum guarantees, minimum guarantee plus revenue share, revenue share, flat acquisition fees, exclusive or non-exclusive licences, perpetual or limited-term licences, first or second windows, bundle or slate licensing, per-title, per-episode, per-season, per-view, per-stream, usage-based, and specialised AI dataset or model-evaluation licensing.

## Marketplace, Screening and Deal Room

The buyer marketplace is intended to be confidential and available only to verified buyers with appropriate role, company, territory, and commercial eligibility. StreamVista should expose only buyer-safe catalogue information and must not expose private creator data, internal notes, legal documents, permanent master-file URLs, or confidential pricing information belonging to unrelated transactions.

Secure screener access should be buyer-specific, expiring, revocable, and audited. The concept supports visible watermarking, session tracking, access logging, and secure cloud delivery mechanisms using providers such as Oracle Cloud Infrastructure and AWS where appropriate.

The Deal Room is designed as an **admin-managed commercial workspace**, not an uncontrolled creator-buyer chat system. It supports enquiries, qualification, rights selection, territories, languages, exclusivity, commercial terms, offers, counter-offers, negotiation rounds, internal notes, legal review, agreement versions, attachments, approvals, and status history.

## Agreements, Payments and License Activation

The concept includes digital agreement generation, revision control, legal review, creator and buyer review, version locking, digital signing, signed-document storage, amendments, extensions, termination, and renewal.

Payment infrastructure may integrate with gateways such as **Razorpay**, but payment-provider integration alone is not considered the complete commercial workflow. The wider architecture includes invoices, payment verification, webhook idempotency, reconciliation, failed-payment handling, partial or instalment payments, taxes, receipts, financial permissions, and audit evidence.

A licence should become active only after the required conditions—such as signed agreement, verified rights, payment conditions, tax requirements, and compliance clearance—are satisfied.

## Secure Delivery, Revenue and Renewal

Master delivery is distinct from screener access. Licensed assets should be made available only to entitled buyers using secure, expiring, revocable access. The concept supports controlled download links and cloud-to-cloud delivery through OCI, AWS, or other compatible storage systems, together with delivery manifests, entitlement validation, access logs, acceptance, rejection, and completion evidence.

Revenue operations include buyer reports, import validation, duplicate detection, reconciliation, revenue lines, royalty allocations, StreamVista commission, creator share calculations, statements, approval, creator acknowledgement, and payout. Revenue analytics may be presented per title, buyer, territory, platform, licensing model, and reporting period.

Renewal management includes monitoring upcoming expiry dates, revalidating rights and conflicts, negotiating extensions or amendments, collecting signatures and payments, and revoking expired access when necessary.

## AI-Assisted and MCP-Based Operations

StreamVista Cloud X may incorporate AI-assisted operational tools and Model Context Protocol (MCP) integrations to inspect, validate, audit, plan, and—only under explicit approval—perform controlled actions across source code, business workflows, databases, rights, payments, delivery, security, and operational systems. The AI/MCP layer is intended to remain governed by role-based permissions, audit logging, explicit approval gates, and default-deny principles.
